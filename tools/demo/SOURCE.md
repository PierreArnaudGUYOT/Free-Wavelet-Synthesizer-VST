# Demo music source

The demo piece comes from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org) (free sheet music typeset with
GNU LilyPond; MIDI file generated from the score). `node tools/make_demo.js` converts the first part of the piece
(from the phrase after the opening chords, cut at a natural phrase, about 2 minutes) into `web/js/demo_data.js`.

| File | Piece | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `appassionata3.mid` | L. van Beethoven — Piano Sonata No. 23 "Appassionata", Op. 57, III. Allegro ma non troppo – Presto | Public Domain | [LVB_Sonate_57_3.mid](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/O57/LVB_Sonate_57_3/LVB_Sonate_57_3.mid) |

Notes
- The final version of the demo plays this one piece (▶ DEMO, a tour of the presets). The other songs of the earlier betas
  (Bach, Beethoven's *Moonlight*, *Rage over a Lost Penny*, Symphony No. 5, *Pathétique*, *The Tempest*, Op. 111), the song menu and
  the loading of a MIDI file were removed at the owner's request; so was the CC BY-SA 2.5 *Moonlight* data.
- Copyrighted songs are not bundled.
