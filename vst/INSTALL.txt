OktoWave 1.0 (Okto MultiWave Synth, version 1.0.15) - VST3 / VST2 instrument for Windows 10/11 (64-bit) (macOS: OktoMultiWaveSynth-1.0.15-mac.zip, same release page)
Created by Pierre Arnaud GUYOT (OktoPierre). Free software under the GNU GPL v3 or later (GPL-3.0-or-later):
the licence text is in OktoMultiWaveSynth.vst3\Contents\Resources\LICENSE.txt.
Source code: https://github.com/PierreArnaudGUYOT/Okto-MultiWave-Synth

Three oscillators (A1 A2 subtractive, B1 additive), three LFOs, a real filter (LADDER / OTA),
208 presets (the author's choice), a chord mode with bass, an oriental keyboard.

NEW IN 1.0.15
- A CLEANER OUTPUT STAGE: a look-ahead limiter replaces the peak guard and the tanh soft limiter. The old stage bent every peak above -3 dBFS (a slight
  buzz on loud, dense chords); the new one looks 1.5 ms ahead and turns the volume down just before the peak: no sample above -1 dBFS, no tanh.
  Below -3 dBFS (where the old stage did nothing) the sound is exactly the same as in 1.0.14.
- The plug-in now has a fixed latency of 1.5 ms, which it reports to the DAW (Ableton Live compensates it by itself).
- The author's own sounds: the 9 factory presets he adjusted that the demo does not play take his values (A · Shimmering Strings, A · Warm Pad,
  AA · Chalk Edge Call, AB · Resonance Storm, AAB · Static & Fibre, AA · Tide Horns, AAB · Stair & Tap, AA · Sweep of Pixel LGT, AB · Step Spiral LGT).
  The presets of the demo keep their sound.
- 49 presets removed (the ones the author deleted, except those the demo plays): 208 presets now (19 LGT). The order of the lists does not change;
  a Live Set that names a removed preset keeps its sound.
- The credits spell the name right: Jérémie Paligrini (Soul Machine).
  Your presets file, stars and user bank are kept.

NEW IN 1.0.12
- THE SOUND STOPS WHEN THE HOST STOPS. In a DAW such as Ableton Live a sound could stay on after STOP: a sustain pedal still down in the MIDI sequence held the released notes, notes of a stopped
  sequence got no note off, a note released at the moment the pedal goes down was held. The plug-in now releases every note and lifts the pedal when the host stops, understands the MIDI
  messages "all notes off", "all sound off" and "reset all controllers", and puts the pedal at its exact place among the notes.

INSTALL
1. Copy the WHOLE folder OktoMultiWaveSynth.vst3 (the manual is inside it) into ONE of these:
   - your VST3 folder:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3   (needs administrator rights), or
   - your VST2 plug-in folder (the one set as "VST2 Plug-in Custom Folder" in Ableton Live; no administrator rights).
   The same folder works as VST3 and as VST2. Replace the folder of 1.0.14 (or of an older version) if you have it.
2. In Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-ins, switch on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (or "Use VST2 Plug-in Custom Folder")
   and click Rescan.
3. In Live's browser: Plug-ins > OktoPierre > Okto MultiWave Synth. Drag it onto a MIDI track.

It installs next to the Wavelet Synthesizer and the WaveChord Synth (its own name and IDs). A Live Set saved with a beta loads as before
(the parameters saved in the Live Set are kept as they are).

YOUR PRESETS FILE
SAVE PRESET, the user bank, deleted presets, the stars you give and the order of the lists are kept in one readable text file,
"OKTOMULTIWAVE presets.txt", next to the OktoMultiWaveSynth.vst3 folder. That folder must allow writing: the VST3 system
folder needs administrator rights, so either install into a folder you own, or start Live as administrator once.
The file of 1.0 / 1.0.1 is read: your user bank, your stars and the presets you adjusted or deleted are kept; the lists of the browser are rebuilt (sorted, OTHER).
The file of beta 0.3.3 is read: your user bank is kept; your stars, the lists of the browser and the presets you adjusted or deleted (their
numbered names are gone) start again from the final list (sorted, TOP Sounds first).
