Making it move, finishing the sound

The oscillator and its filter make a tone. Envelopes, LFOs, the voice filter and the effects shape it in time.

Tuning, envelope, pitch envelope

The OSC PITCH · VOLUME ADSR card belongs to one oscillator. OCTAVE, SEMI and FINE tune it against the others (AA · Slow Violins tunes A1 six cents sharp and A2 six cents flat, both an octave up). The ENVELOPE (ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN, RELEASE, with its picture) is the oscillator's loudness in time, and the only loudness envelope of a note: a click, a pluck, a strike, a pad. Its attack is a curve that starts steep (half way through the ATTACK time the oscillator is at 65 % of its level), so a short attack bites; each oscillator has its own, which is how two oscillators can play a strike and a body. PITCH ENV starts the oscillator that many semitones (±48) away from the note, and TIME says how long it takes to fall back.

Moving the wavelet controls

  • The TONE ENVELOPE (an ADSR, at the left of the FILTER) moves the tone through each note: it drives ENV AMT of the wavelet filters, ENV>WAV (the morph), ENV of the tables and the ENV of OSC DRIVE. It never sets the loudness: the oscillators' envelopes do.
  • LFO 1 moves, at the rate you choose (RATE, or SYNC to the tempo in the plug-in): PITCH (vibrato), CUTOFF, POSITION, WAVELET (the morph), ATOMS, PEAK and ENV>WAV, each by the amount of its knob. Shapes: sine, triangle, saw, square, sample & hold.
  • LFO 2 and LFO 3 (two tabs of one card) each move one control, chosen with TARGET among 19, in every oscillator that has it, by AMOUNT (negative reverses it). Among them: Source Phase, Sparsity, Source Width, Window Width, Window Depth, Wavelet Morph. A wavelet control that moves makes the timbre swim, which no filter sweep does: this is the SuperModulation of the presets of that name (AA · Deep Ripple).

The rest of the voice

Each note is rendered by its own voice (up to 16, VOICES). Its oscillators are added by OSC MIX (with the SUB OSC, a plain wave two octaves down, one octave down, at the same pitch or one octave up), then pass a conventional resonant FILTER (the OTA, round and smooth; its CUTOFF is in hertz and it has its own ADSR), then VELOCITY (in OSC MIX) sets how much the key's strength matters for the level. UNISON plays 1 to 4 detuned copies (DETUNE in cents) spread by STEREO; GLIDE and the portamento switch (OFF · POLY · LEGATO) slide between notes. All the notes then share the EFFECTS: DRIVE, CHORUS, a ping-pong delay, a reverb, and a soft limiter. PUNCH, at the right of the effects, gives every note a stronger start, most on the low notes: 0 is off, 50 % (every preset) a light punch, 100 % a strong one; it never lowers the level.

Three recipes

A · Warm PadA smooth wavelet (Coiflet 3), CUTOFF 5 with a little PEAK, a quick ENVELOPE ATTACK (5 ms) and a long release (1.4 s), UNISON 3 wide in the stereo field, a little delay and a long reverb.
A · Wave BrassDaubechies 20, CUTOFF 3.3, SLOPE 36, PEAK 8; ENV AMT +4 so that every note opens the filter by four bands as the TONE ENVELOPE rises (0.06 s); MORPH and ENV>WAV carry the timbre towards the second wavelet.
AA · Drum Bloom LGTA2 is a sine one octave up, with a short ENVELOPE (decay 0.19 s, sustain 0) and a PITCH ENV of +16 semitones in 50 ms: the thump of a drum. A1 (a square) and the slow LFOs bloom around it.
