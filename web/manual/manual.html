OKTOWAVE

Okto MultiWave Synth — a polyphonic synthesizer whose oscillators are built from wavelets. This manual is one page: read it top to bottom once, then use the menu.

1 · Start in one minute

In the browser

  1. Open the web app (Chrome or Edge; web/index.html also runs from a file).
  2. Click Play the synth, or Run the demo for a tour of the sounds.
  3. Play the on-screen keyboard with the mouse, the keys 1…0 and A S D F…, or a MIDI keyboard. Z / X change the octave.

As a plug-in

  1. Download the zip from the Releases page and unzip it.
  2. Install it (see Install), rescan the plug-ins in your DAW.
  3. Load OktoPierre → Okto MultiWave Synth on a MIDI track. Same sound, same panel.

2 · The idea

A normal oscillator repeats one long, smooth wave. A wavelet is a tiny wave that starts, rings for a moment and fades: it lives in one place and one moment. OKTOWAVE makes the oscillator itself out of wavelets, like bricks of sound: big bricks make the body of the note, small bricks make its shine.

  • A1 and A2 are subtractive. They start from a classic wave (saw, square, pulse, triangle, sine, noise), cut it into wavelet bricks, and then keep, remove or move bricks.
  • B1 is additive. It builds the sound brick by brick: 11 drawbars, one per size of brick.
  • The filter works on bricks. The SCALE FILTER keeps or removes bricks by size, and the POSITION window confines it to one part of the cycle: something a classic filter cannot do.
  • You choose the wavelet: Haar, Daubechies 2 / 4 / 8 / 20, Symlet 8 or Coiflet 3. A blocky wavelet sounds raw, a smooth one sounds soft.

After the oscillators every voice goes through a conventional resonant low-pass FILTER, the AMP ENVELOPE, then the shared EFFECTS: saturation, chorus, ping-pong delay, reverb.

3 · The panel

The panel is the same in the browser and in the plug-in, except for a few buttons (listed at the end of this section). From top to bottom:

HeaderThe preset box (◀ ▶ step through the sounds, a click on the name opens the preset browser), MANUAL, the master volume knob and the level meter. Browser only: ▶ DEMO and DOWNLOAD VST. Plug-in only: SAVE PRESET and the window-size button.
OSC1 A1 · OSC2 A2 · OSC3 B1The three folder tabs choose which oscillator's cards you see. They never change the sound: a lit dot says the oscillator is heard, and what you hear is set by OSC MIX.
WAVELETThe heart of an oscillator. On A: the wave buttons, ATOM SHAPE (one brick of the chosen wavelet), SOURCE CYCLE, WAVELET and WAVELET 2, MORPH (slides from one wavelet to the other), ENV>WAV, PHASE, WIDTH (pulse and triangle), SPARSITY (keeps only the strongest bricks). On B: the PATTERN buttons, the 11 drawbars (one per brick size), TILT, SEED and the band envelope knobs.
SCALE FILTERLow-pass, high-pass, band-pass or band-reject on brick sizes, with cutoff, slope, resonance, key tracking, envelope and velocity amounts.
POSITIONA window that confines the filter (A) or the detail (B) to one part of the cycle. Raise DEPTH to turn it on.
TUNE · OWN ENVELOPE · PITCH ENVELOPEThe oscillator's own octave, semitones and fine tuning; its private ADSR (it can only make the oscillator shorter than the voice: ideal for clicks and plucks); and a pitch envelope that glides the oscillator to the note.
OSC MIX · SUB OSCThe three oscillator levels (an oscillator at 0 costs nothing), and the sub oscillator one or two octaves below.
SCOPE · SCALOGRAMThe card SCOPE · ALL OSCILLATORS shows one cycle of the whole sound; under it the SCALOGRAM shows its bricks in the 11 sizes: rows are sizes, bright cells are loud bricks. Both move with the sound.
FILTERA 24 dB resonant low-pass in every voice, LADDER (dark, gritty) or OTA (round, smooth), with its own ADSR. Open by default.
AMP ENVELOPE · MOD ENVELOPELoudness of the voice (and VELOCITY); the MOD ENVELOPE moves the scale cutoffs and the wavelet morph.
LFO 1 · LFO 2/3LFO 1 moves pitch, cutoff, position, morph, sparsity and resonance. LFO 2 and LFO 3 (two tabs) each move one control of your choice (TARGET) in every oscillator at once. Moving a wavelet control makes the timbre swim in a way a classic filter sweep cannot.
VOICEPortamento (OFF · POLY · LEGATO), octave, fine tune, VOICES (1 = mono), GLIDE, UNISON copies, DETUNE, STEREO spread, and the voice LEDs.
EFFECTSSaturation (DRIVE), a triple chorus, a ping-pong delay and a reverb, in that order, then the master volume and a soft limiter that keeps the output below 0 dBFS.
PLAYCHORD MODE and the TUNING switch (WEST · ARAB · TURK): see Playing.
KeyboardFive octaves, with the pitch and mod wheels at its left. Lit keys follow what is played, by you, by MIDI or by the demo.

Working with the controls

  • Drag a knob up or down to change it; hold Shift for five times finer steps. The mouse wheel also works.
  • Double-click (or Ctrl / Cmd + click) resets a control to its default. Hover a knob to read its value.
  • Right-click a control in the browser to link a MIDI knob (MIDI learn; Esc cancels).
  • A greyed-out control has no effect with the current settings (for example WIDTH unless the wave is PULSE or TRI).
  • Browser only: Save preset, Export, Import in the bar under the panel. Plug-in only: the SYNC buttons that lock the delay and the LFOs to the tempo of your DAW.

4 · Playing

CHORD MODE

The CHORD MODE button of the PLAY card turns the keyboard into a chord harp and shows the CHORD KEYBOARD card under it. One octave of keys (KEYS OCT, tinted on the keyboard) chooses the chord type and makes no sound; every key above it plays the whole chord with one finger. The twelve pads show the types (Maj, Min, 7, m7, Maj7, Sus2, Sus4, Dim, Aug, 5, Add9, Note); a click on a pad chooses one. VOICING stacks the chord or folds it into one octave, STRUM starts its notes one after the other, the strum plate strums it with the mouse, and BASS adds the root two octaves below. The chord follows your left hand: change the type while a note is held and the held chord changes at once. These are playing settings, never part of a preset.

Oriental tunings

The TUNING switch gives the keyboard quarter tones. WEST is equal temperament. ARAB plays 24 tones to the octave; TURK plays the 53 commas of Turkish music. Outside WEST the card shows MAQAM (the scale: Rast, Bayati, Saba, Hijaz…) and TONIC (the note it starts on); small amber arrows mark the keys the tuning moves, and the panel takes sand-gold (ARAB) or carmine (TURK) colours with an oriental pattern on the PLAY card. The demo always plays in equal temperament.

The demo

In the browser, ▶ DEMO plays Beethoven's Appassionata while the sounds change every few seconds; its window presents the synthesizer, one sentence per sound. ■ STOP (or Esc) stops it and puts every control back.

5 · Presets

OktoWave has 237 presets, all kept by its author after his own listening. TOP Sounds come first (the best rated first), then twelve sections: Pad, SuperModulation, Bass, Lead, Keyboard, Organ, Strings, Brass & Wind, Bells & Mallets, FX, Drums & Perc and Other. The letters at the start of a name say which oscillators it uses (A, AA, AB, AAB, B).

  • Choose a sound with ◀ ▶, or click the preset name to open the preset browser at the right of the panel: one click plays a sound, the arrow keys ↑ ↓ ← → move in the lists. The browser shows the author's stars (1 to 10) at the right of each sound.
  • Browser: Save preset keeps your sound in this browser, Export writes a .json file, Import reads one.
  • Plug-in: SAVE PRESET adjusts the sound playing (RESTORE brings the original back) or replaces a sound of your USER BANK. In the browser panel you can move, delete, export and import sounds, and rate them with your own stars. The USER BANK starts with a KeyAttac folder of very percussive favourites. Everything lives in one readable file, OKTOMULTIWAVE presets.txt, kept next to the .vst3 folder.

6 · Install the plug-in

Windows (VST3 · VST2)

  1. Copy the whole OktoMultiWaveSynth.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (administrator rights), or into your VST2 folder.
  2. Rescan in your DAW (Ableton Live: Preferences → Plug-ins → Rescan).
  3. The presets file is written next to that folder, so it must allow writing: install in a folder you own if you cannot write there.

macOS (VST3, Apple silicon and Intel)

  1. Copy OktoMultiWaveSynth.vst3 into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (your own folder, so the presets file can be written).
  2. The plug-in is free and not signed with a paid Apple certificate: allow it once with xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/OktoMultiWaveSynth.vst3 in Terminal, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Rescan in your DAW. Logic Pro and GarageBand only load Audio Units and cannot use it. On a Mac, Cmd-click resets a knob and Control-click is a right-click.

7 · Help

No sound in the browser

Browsers only allow sound after a click: click Play the synth in the welcome window. The status bar must say Audio on. Only one tab plays at a time.

I click an oscillator tab and the sound does not change

By design: the tabs only choose which cards you see. The sound is set by the three levels in OSC MIX.

A knob has no effect

Check the level of the oscillator you are editing in OSC MIX (an oscillator at 0 is silent) and whether the control is greyed out. With the FILTER wide open, its cutoff does nothing.

It crackles, or the page slows down

The cost grows with VOICES × UNISON × the oscillators that are on. Lower UNISON or VOICES, turn off oscillators you do not hear, open the FILTER, or use the plug-in, which has the whole computer's time. A few sounds with long releases and many unison copies are heavy on a slow computer.

The plug-in does not appear in my DAW

Copy the whole bundle folder (not only the file inside), then rescan. On Windows the DAW must see the VST3 (or VST2) folder you used.

SAVE PRESET says it cannot write

The presets file sits next to the .vst3 folder, which must allow writing. Install the plug-in in a folder you own, or run your DAW as administrator once.
